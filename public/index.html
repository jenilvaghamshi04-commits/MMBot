Bot settings

The bot starts only when you press ON. Live exchange trading is not connected.

Depth shortfalls are divided into random amounts across the selected number of orders, starting at the outer 2% edge and stepping inward by the depth order tick size. Normal order tick size controls the ladder and spread. Automatic sells start at the DEX price rounded up to a normal tick; buys start one configured spread below. Small-order min/max and skip limits apply only to ladder orders.

Sell depth order: highest buy × 1.02. Buy depth order: lowest sell × 0.98. Depth prices round inward to the depth tick size. For 5 divisions and $500 missing depth, five random amounts total $500. Sizes are retained between cycles and scale when the required depth changes. Existing depth reduces the missing amount before division. Missing depth is split across adjacent prices inward from that boundary, regardless of small-order size limits or occupied ladder prices. Buy orders never exceed the DEX price; sell orders never go below it. Existing orders count toward targets. Invalid bot orders are removed on the next ON cycle; manual orders stay untouched.

Example: DEX 0.0000200 caps buys at 0.0000200 and floors sells at 0.0000200. Actual 2% limits use the opposite first order. Spread 1 means adjacent prices: buy 0.0000219 and sell 0.0000220 when DEX is 0.0000220 and normal tick is 0.0000001.
